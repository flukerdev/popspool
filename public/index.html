The rules

Everyone predicts the regular-season record of their own alma mater — Georgia, Auburn, or Ole Miss. Closest to the real record wins. One entry per person.

When people tie on the record, three score predictions break it: Georgia–Auburn, Georgia–Ole Miss, and the Iron Bowl. If a tie somehow survives all three, the SEC champion pick settles it.

Commissioner's clarification: tiebreakers are scored on the point spread differential. If Georgia beats Auburn by 14 and you predicted 13, you are off by one — not off by the total points. Your three margin errors are added together, and the smallest total wins.
Projections from 60,000 simulations. Odds and ratings via CollegeFootballData.
